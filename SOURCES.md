# Sources and verification notes

Every story on the front page broke or developed on Sunday,
22 November 2026 (Japan time), and each one was confirmed in at
least two independent outlets before it was drawn. This file lists
what was used, so a reader can retrace the page.

## Lead story — COP31 in Antalya

Reuters reporting on Sunday described the final package being put
to almost 200 countries after the summit overran its Friday
deadline. The draft keeps the goal of electricity reaching 35% of
final energy by 2035 and, once again, avoids any explicit mention
of fossil fuels, even though roughly 130 countries had pressed for
phase-out language. CNA carried the same text and figures.

## NATO and Poland

Reuters quoted Secretary General Mark Rutte in Warsaw promising
that NATO will defend every inch of Polish territory, after three
objects — assessed as probably two missiles and a drone — violated
Polish airspace. The North Atlantic Council was set to meet on
Monday in Brussels. President Zelenskiy separately reported that
Russia's overnight strike on Kryvyi Rih killed at least two people,
including a seven-year-old boy.

## Brazil supercell storm

AFP updates on Sunday raised the toll in Tupanciretã, Rio Grande
do Sul, to four dead and 162 injured. Winds reached 114 km/h,
around 8,000 homes were damaged, and almost all of the town's
roughly 20,000 residents were displaced.

## Nigeria funeral boat

The Associated Press and Al Jazeera both reported that a boat
carrying mourners from Ayama to Ukubie in Bayelsa State capsized
after a mechanical failure. At least 24 people died, 24 were
rescued, and the search was still going on when the page was drawn.

## Mexico cartel capture

Reuters, CNN, the Wall Street Journal and CNA all carried the
capture of Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as "El Chapo Isidro",
in Guasave, Sinaloa. He was on the FBI's Ten Most Wanted list with
a five-million-dollar reward. Gun battles during the operation
killed eleven people — four soldiers and seven gunmen — and
burning roadblocks were reported around the town.

## United States intrusions

AFP reported on Sunday that the FBI was investigating a suspected
intrusion into the NCIC criminal database, the second FBI breach
since September (also covered by CNN). NBC reported that a
Department of Agriculture system handling farmer payments had been
breached as well; whether the intrusions are linked was not known.

## Items checked and left off the page

A few tempting stories turned out to belong to other days and were
excluded: the Peru earthquake was Friday local time, and the
International Court of Justice ruling on Myanmar dated from
19 November. Keeping the page to one honest day mattered more than
filling space.
